  - **Flash Proxy Index Module** (`backend/cv/flash_proxy.py`):
    - Dual-exposure differential reflectance (ΔI = I_flash - I_ambient) capturing near-visible red edge (~680–720 nm) ([Nicolaï-2007], [Taniwaki-2023]).

    - Detects cuticular water congestion, tissue lysis, and early soft rot prior to foul liquid oozing.
    - Generates 8-bit false-color colormap heatmaps (JET / INFERNO) with base64 JPEG encoding.
    - `POST /api/v1/inspections/{id}/fpi` endpoint for dual-exposure analysis.
