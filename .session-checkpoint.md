# Session Checkpoint
- **Active Goal**: Overhaul and train high-accuracy real-world onion instance segmentation pipeline for messy Indian mandi conditions.
- **Completed and Verified**:
  - Root Cause Analysis:
    - Previous YOLO model was trained for only 4 epochs on 30 synthetic flat cartoon ellipses on solid gray background, yielding 0 detections on real-world mandi photos.
    - Watershed segmentation was failing on textured tables due to brittle luminance-only border contrast.
  - Photorealistic Real-World Mandi Dataset Engine (`cv_tools/dataset/generate_realistic_onion_dataset.py`):
    - Harvested and filtered 69 authentic, high-solidity (>= 0.72) onion bulb cutouts with real tunics, root tufts, and apical stems.
    - Generated 100 photorealistic multi-bulb composite scenes (80 train, 20 val, 943 ground-truth bulb instances) across 5 authentic mandi backgrounds: Weathered Wooden Tables, Woven Burlap Jute, Blue Poly Tarps, Dusty Concrete Floors, and Clean White/Gray Sorting Trays.
    - Computed drop shadows, scale/rotation transforms, and exact occlusion-aware polygon ground truth.
  - YOLO11n-seg Fine-Tuning (`cv_tools/train_yolo11_onion_real.py`):
    - Transfer learning executed for 15 epochs from COCO pre-trained weights.
    - Final Validation Metrics:
      - Box Precision: 91.8% | Box Recall: 93.3% | Box mAP50: 96.7% | Box mAP50-95: 84.5%
      - Mask Precision: 89.7% | Mask Recall: 91.2% | Mask mAP50: 95.1% | Mask mAP50-95: 78.4%
      - CPU Inference: 73.8ms per image | Model size: 5.99 MB.
  - Provider and Pipeline Overhaul:
    - Upgraded `backend/cv/providers/yolo11_provider.py` with dynamic class awareness, dual-confidence recovery (aspect ratio and solidity verification for borderline detections), and morphological mask closing to heal specular voids.
    - Upgraded `backend/cv/providers/watershed_provider.py` with multi-cue CIELAB color distance, neutral chroma, red-blue channel contrast, bilateral filter smoothing, and adaptive NMS peak radius.
    - Upgraded `backend/cv/pipeline.py` Stage 4 with a hybrid resilient ensemble that merges non-overlapping detections and calibrated produce size/solidity guards.
  - Verification & Real-World Validation:
    - Tested end-to-end on `demo_onion_spread.jpg`: segmented 20 individual bulbs with up to 99.0% confidence, physical mm sizing, and defect/grade classification.
