THE NATIONAL PROBLEM STATEMENT

Post-Harvest Losses & Buffer Integrity in India's Onion Value Chain

India produces over 30 million tonnes of onions annually (NHB). Under the Price Stabilisation Fund (PSF), procurement agencies (NAFED and NCCF) procure strategic buffer stocks. In August 2026, widespread controversies erupted at Lasalgaon during "Kanda Express" rail buffer loading, where farmers and traders alleged that 10–20 mm undersized bulbs and decaying rot were loaded directly into central buffer stocks due to visual-only subjective inspections. ICAR-DOGR studies show 20–30% storage losses from latent fungal decay and desiccation.
